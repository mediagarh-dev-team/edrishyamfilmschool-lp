Your FilmMaking Journey Starts With Learning The Craft.

A film is more than what appears on screen.
It takes a story, a vision, technical skills and a team to bring it to life.

The Diploma in Advanced Filmmaking at e-Drishyam introduces you to the creative and technical processes behind filmmaking. Explore how ideas develop into stories, how visuals are captured and how footage comes together to create a film.

Explore the creative and technical disciplines that come together to make a film.

Scriptwriting & Storytelling

Learn how to develop ideas, build narratives, create characters and write stories for the screen.

Cinematography

Understand camera techniques, framing, composition, lenses, lighting and visual storytelling.

Film Direction

Explore the role of a director, scene planning, visualisation and the process of bringing a story to life.

Editing & Post-Production

Learn how to shape footage into a cohesive story through editing and post-production techniques.

Sound Design & Music

Discover how sound, dialogue and music shape mood and storytelling on screen.

Filmmaking Practice

Plan shoots, work with a crew and apply your learning on hands-on film projects.
